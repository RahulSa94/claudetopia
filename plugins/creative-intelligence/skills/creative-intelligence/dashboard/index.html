Every number is judged against creatives of the same campaign type (objective), optimisation goal and format, so a lead-form video is never compared with a traffic static. "Type avg" is that group's average. Green = 20%+ better, red = 20%+ worse. Hold = ThruPlays ÷ 3-second views. Reach is the sum of each ad's reach, so it can count one person twice. Lead-goal verdicts use the project's target CPL; Test = under 2× target CPL spent; Review before pausing = would be Fix-Pause but it is the top spender or top lead source in its campaign type. Diagnosis (videos with 1,000+ impressions): 1 · keep the opening, recut the body · 2 · swap only the first 3 seconds · 3 · fix CTA or offer · 4 · concept fails · 5 · new angle.

This period's skill

Hook versus hold. A video can fail at the opening or in the body, and each needs the opposite fix. Read the two numbers separately before calling a video good or bad.

15-minute exercise: pick one Scenario 1 or 2 video above and write three new first-3-second openings, keeping everything else the same.

Change one thing at a time. When a creative is close to target, change only the hook, only the body or only the CTA, so the next read shows which change worked.

15-minute exercise: take one Iterate creative and write a brief that changes a single element.

How this works, and what it leaves out
  • Free core: Meta's Ads MCP (connector "metamcp"), read only. In Live it runs with each viewer's own Meta access, or the owner's where the owner shares the connector.
  • Target CPL per project = monthly spend target ÷ monthly lead target. Leads are what Meta reports, not CRM-qualified leads.
  • Spend check: ad-level spend is compared with ad-set spend; a gap over 2% is flagged.
  • —
  • Stored pulls (the Data picker) come from a dated store kept in the owner's Google Drive (Google Drive MCP), so people without connectors see the numbers. Every pull is kept.
  • Thumbnails are stored in the dashboard, because dashboard pages may not load images from Facebook.
  • Format: Video if the ad has video plays; Carousel if it has carousel cards or "carousel" in its name; otherwise Static.
